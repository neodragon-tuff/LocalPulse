How it works

From bureaucratic PDF
to plain English — every week

Three things happen automatically so you never have to dig through city websites again.

01 / GATHER

We scrape the public record

Our system automatically pulls city council minutes, zoning filings, building permits, police blotters, and school board decisions from official government portals — sorted by your ZIP code.

02 / UNDERSTAND

AI reads the boring parts

An AI model reads the full documents, extracts what actually matters for residents, filters out bureaucratic filler, and writes a clear, plain-English summary of each item — with no spin.

03 / DELIVER

Lands in your inbox Monday

Every Monday morning, your digest arrives with everything that happened in your neighborhood that week — categorized, sourced, and linked back to the original document so you can verify anything.

New 8-story residential building approved for 6th & Folsom

A project adding 94 units (18 affordable) was approved by the Planning Commission after two years of review. Construction expected to begin Q1 2027 and complete in 2029.

Approved SF Planning Commission · Case 2024-003471

Proposed rezoning of Bryant St corridor from industrial to mixed-use

The city is considering changing zoning designations for 14 parcels between 5th and 8th St to allow ground-floor retail. Public comment period opens July 1.

In progress SF Planning Dept · Notice 2026-0147

🚔 Public Safety

Auto break-in cluster reported near 4th & King — 11 incidents last week

SFPD district station logged an uptick in vehicle break-ins between Howard and Townsend. Officers increased patrols and arrested one suspect on June 14.

Alert SFPD Incident Reports · Week of Jun 9

🏛 City Council & Policy

Board approved $2.4M for new SoMa streetscape improvements

Budget includes new trees, wider sidewalks, and protected bike lanes on Howard St between 2nd and 5th. Design process begins in September, completion targeted for Spring 2028.

Funded SF Board of Supervisors · Resolution 26-0821

Parking enforcement hours extended to midnight on weekends

Starting July 1, meter enforcement in SoMa and Mission will run until midnight on Fridays and Saturdays. Free parking on Sundays remains unchanged.

Effective soon SFMTA · Policy Update Jun 10, 2026
